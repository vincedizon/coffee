About Silung Coffee

Silung Coffee isn't just a café—it's a cultural hub in Mabalacat, offering well-loved drinks, artisanal pastries, and a vibrant space that blends heritage, community, and creativity.

Located inside the Tabnuan Cultural Center along MacArthur Highway in Poblacion, our café brings you a nostalgic yet modern atmosphere—perfect for quiet afternoons or lively weekend hangouts.

Whether you're looking to enjoy a sea-salt latte, discover local art, or simply unwind in a historic setting, Silung Coffee is worth a visit.

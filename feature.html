Our Story

Why choose Silung Coffee?

We believe a great cup of coffee is just the beginning. At Silung, we've built a space where culture, community, and creativity thrive together — right in the heart of Mabalacat.

01
Authentic Local Sourcing

We partner with local farmers in the Cordillera region to bring you single-origin Arabica beans, ensuring every cup supports local communities and offers a taste of the Philippines' rich coffee heritage.

02
A Hub for Art & Culture

Nestled within the Tabnuan Cultural Center, our café doubles as an art gallery. We feature rotating exhibits from local artists, poets, and musicians — a space where creativity flourishes.

03
Handcrafted Signature Drinks

Our skilled baristas craft a range of unique signature beverages, including our popular Sea-Salt Latte and Pandan Cold Brew — a fusion of traditional Filipino flavors and modern coffee artistry.

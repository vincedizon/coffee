About Us

More than
just a café

Silung Coffee is a vibrant space that blends heritage, community, and creativity. Located inside the Tabnuan Cultural Center along MacArthur Highway in Poblacion, our café brings you a nostalgic yet modern atmosphere.

Whether you're looking to enjoy a sea-salt latte, discover local art, or simply unwind in a historic setting, Silung Coffee is worth a visit.
